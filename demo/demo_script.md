# Demo Script – NoteWise Smart Notes Summarizer (3-4 minutes)

## 1. Hook (20 seconds)
"Students have too many notes and too little time to revise. NoteWise uses
AI to compress, quiz, and search your notes — everything stays local."

## 2. Load Sample Data (15 seconds)
- Open the app at `http://localhost:8501`
- Click **Load sample data** on the home page
- Show the 6 notes loaded across DBMS, OS, and ML

## 3. Summarize (45 seconds)
- Navigate to **Summarize**
- Select "Normalization (DBMS)" from the dropdown
- Choose "exam" summary style
- Click **Summarize**
- Show: summary with highlighted key terms, compression bar ("Compressed 2,400 words to 190"), reading time saved, topic chips
- Open the flashcards expander

## 4. Quiz (30 seconds)
- Navigate to **Study** → Quiz tab
- Select the same note
- Answer the 5 MCQs
- Submit → show "3 of 5 correct" with per-question explanations
- Score is saved to the database automatically

## 5. Chat (45 seconds)
- Navigate to **Ask Notes**
- Type: "What is the difference between 2NF and 3NF?"
- Show the answer with source citations and relevance scores
- Ask a follow-up: "Give me an example of a transitive dependency"

## 6. Dashboard (45 seconds)
- Navigate to **Dashboard**
- Show 4 KPIs at the top
- Point out: notes per subject chart, quiz score trend
- Highlight the weak topics table: "OS Deadlocks flagged at 30% average"
- Read the recommendations list

## 7. Report (20 seconds)
- Still on Dashboard, scroll to Export
- Download the study sheet for the normalization note
- Show it opens as clean Markdown

## 8. Close (20 seconds)
"All AI runs locally via Ollama — no internet needed, no data leaves the laptop.
Future: spaced repetition, multi-language, LMS integration."
